## General Tips:

1. **Resolution:** Generate at 512x512 or higher, then crop to square
2. **File Format:** PNG for transparency support
3. **Naming:** Save as `{preset_name}_avatar.png` (e.g., `yuki_avatar.png`)
4. **Location:** Place in `static/avatars/` directory
5. **Style Consistency:** Use same anime style across all avatars
6. **Face Focus:** Ensure face is clearly visible and centered
7. **Background:** Keep backgrounds simple but thematic
